# Open-source license decision required

Do not publish the repository as open source without choosing and adding a real license.

Common choices to evaluate:
- MIT: permissive, short, broad reuse.
- Apache-2.0: permissive with explicit patent grant and additional notices.
- GPL-3.0: copyleft; derivative distributed software must comply with GPL terms.

Choose based on the project's goals and dependency licenses. Add the complete official license text only after the decision is made.
